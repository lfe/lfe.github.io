## Colours and gradations

Each row shows a named paint with its variable, hexadecimal value, and actual quantized OKLCH coordinates. A family stop is a useful choice in a palette; it does not promise a contrast level or uniform perceptual step. The exact role anchors are listed separately because they are not substitutes for the nearest numbered stop.

## How the wheel was chosen

The adopted panel is a thirty-degree OKLCH hue arrangement around the dark slate-800 anchor. Lightness and chroma vary with purpose, gamut limits can reduce chroma, and conversion to 8-bit sRGB shifts the measured hue. Equal angles are design choices, not equal perceptual distances or a universal harmony rule.
