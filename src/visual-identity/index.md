## A working visual reference

This is a draft for practical conversations about how LFE appears across its site and tools. Colour values below are read from the current stylesheet and palette file; the reference is meant to help us discuss use, not to announce a finished identity policy.

The five short sections each answer a different question: which paints are available, what jobs they do, how the light and dark directions behave, and what remains to decide about marks and type.
